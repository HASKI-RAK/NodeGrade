# ADR-0011: Direct browser access restored

Status: accepted. Supersedes the withdrawal of anonymous browser workspaces in ADR-0010; everything else in ADR-0010 stays in force.

The conference workshop that ADR-0010 served is over, and NodeGrade is a directly usable application again. A browser that opens the main URL gets its own `BROWSER` workspace from `POST /api/workspaces`, minted behind the same per-address `WorkspaceCreationThrottle` as the workshop join, resolved by its bearer token like any other workspace, and swept by retention after 60 idle days. The template gallery (`GET /api/templates`, `?kind=WORKFLOW|BLOCK`, a template by slug, a revision by id) serves both kinds to any workspace, and `POST /api/workflows/from-template` copies a published workflow template's current revision into the caller's workspace. Both stay workspace-scoped: the credential is cheap for the browser and keeps grading content and provider budget behind something that is at least rate-limited. From ADR-0010, these stand unchanged: a workshop participant reaches templates as entries of their workshop, each resolved to its pinned revision or the template's newest one when started; a closed or expired workshop is read-only for its participants over HTTP and at run time; the join is throttled per address. The workshop surface moves under the `/workshop` route in the frontend and the titlebar links to it, so the two entry paths are visible side by side rather than one hiding the other.
